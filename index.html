THANATAPATHY is a limited literary magazine made by Coraline Ismael Karim. This page serves as Karim's Capstone Project for the Chesapeake Bay Trust's Chesapeake Conservation and Climate Corps under her host organization "Bikemore". The program and project are meant to portray how Karim's service in the corps program moves forward their goal of a climate-forward world that betters the health of our ecosystems and people alike.
